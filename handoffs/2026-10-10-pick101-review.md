# pick-101 integration review

Source: [`ggand0/pick-101`](https://github.com/ggand0/pick-101), inspected at
`c9d5400ffa0b742fe58a65750fed7982008450d9` on 2026-10-10.

## What its 2 million-step result measures

The public README reports 100% success at 2 million frames for image-based
DrQ-v2. Its `configs/image_based/drqv2_lift_s3_v19.yaml` specifies eight
training environments, 2 million frames, 10 evaluation episodes, three
stacked 84×84 wrist-camera frames, 18 proprioceptive values, and a
200-step episode. The scene has a single 3 cm cube. Curriculum stage 3 resets
the open gripper 3 cm above the cube, with the cube randomized ±2 cm in XY.
The policy outputs Cartesian XYZ increments and a gripper action. The
reward v19 shapes distance from both fingers, two-pad grasp, lift height,
drop avoidance, and short hold time.
The stage-3 reset randomizes cube XY by ±2 cm; the inspected scene and
training configuration do not randomize object shape, mass, friction,
actuator lag, camera pose, or lighting. Its reported evaluation therefore
does not establish robustness to those changes.

The image-training factory uses the environment's default `hold_steps=10`
and does not pass a larger hold setting. Each environment step advances ten
MuJoCo steps at 0.002 s, so its lift success requires about 0.2 s above
8 cm while two-pad contact persists. This is a **lift** result. Its separate
`configs/pick_place.yaml` defines a place task, but the README provides no
placement success result. The public checkout has no trained `.pt` snapshot
or evaluation JSON/video for independent replay. The reported percentage is
therefore a source claim, not a result reproduced in this repo.

The README estimates about eight hours for its 2 million-frame image run.
Its dependency file targets ROCm PyTorch and a forked Gymnasium, and training
depends on its `external/robobase` submodule. A CUDA RTX 5070 Ti run would
require an isolated environment and adapted dependency setup. Its 640×480
wrist rendering across eight processes also needs a VRAM check before that
configuration is used on a 12 GB laptop GPU.

## What was adopted here

- The finger-pad model and contact solver became the optional
  `pick101_contact` physics variant, with MIT attribution. Full mesh contacts
  are disabled in this tabletop adaptation; both pads remain active.
- `grasped_once` in that variant requires simultaneous object contact with
  both pads, matching pick-101's grasp definition.
- The scripted controller was measured on 15 held-out robust contact episodes:
  2/15 pad grasps, 1/15 lifts, and 0/15 placements. The assisted-grasp scene
  is substantially easier and cannot stand in for this contact result.
- The selected camera-and-joint ACT checkpoint was evaluated on the same 15
  contact episodes: 0/15 two-pad grasps, lifts, or placements. It was trained
  on the assisted scene, so this is a transfer failure rather than a trained
  contact-policy result.
- The source's nominal top-down example was run headless with MuJoCo 3.15.0.
  It contacted and lifted the cube, then slipped during the hold and returned
  failure. Its lockfile pins MuJoCo 3.4.0; that difference may matter.

## Next simulation experiment

Use the contact model to train **grasp and sustained lift** before attempting
placement, then evaluate the same fixed robust seeds. Retain the two-pad
reward idea and stage-3 start as a curriculum step, but progress to a stage-4
reach from farther away and then train release into the tray. Compare under
the same object-position, mass, friction, actuator, and camera variations
used by the LeRobot dataset. Record separate success rates for all three
objects and for actual placement. A camera on the real SO-101 must be
calibrated before using pick-101's wrist-camera pose; its pose was tuned for
an innoMaker camera and is not a validated XIAO mounting transform.

The current ACT checkpoint uses an overhead image and simulated joint-target
actions; pick-101's DrQ-v2 policy uses a wrist image and Cartesian actions.
Their weights and action heads are not interchangeable. A new DrQ-v2 run
would be a separate policy experiment, not additional epochs of ACT.
