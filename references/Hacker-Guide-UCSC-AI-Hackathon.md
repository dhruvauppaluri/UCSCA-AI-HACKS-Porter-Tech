CLOUD  AI  ·  LED  BY  CISCO
 Agentic  workflows  that  live  in  the  cloud.  Orchestration,  tool  use,  and  multi-agent  systems  at  scale.  Good  fit  if:  your  idea  needs  more  than  one  machine,  or  has  to  call  other  services.

 LOCAL  AI  ·  LED  BY  ASUS
 AI  that  runs  on  hardware  you  can  carry.  Projects  in  this  track  must  be  built  on  an  ASUS  Ascent  GX10,  which  comes  pre-loaded  with  NVIDIA  Nemotron.  Good  fit  if:  latency,  privacy,  or  working  with  no  network  is  the  point.

 PHYSICAL  AI  ·  LED  BY  AMD
 Agents  with  bodies.  Robotics,  robotic  arms,  and  machines  that  act  on  the  world.  Good  fit  if:  the  demo  only  lands  when  something  actually  moves.

How  judging  works
 Each  team  will  get  a  chance  to  pitch  to  a  group  of  judges.  More  information  will  be  on  Discord.  •  Judging  starts  sharply  at  200  PM.  Each  pitch  is  3  minutes  of  presentation  and  3  minutes  of  Q&A .  A  workshop
on

 •  Stay  at  your  tables  until  judging  is  over.  •  Top  teams  go  to  the  finals  and  pitch  again.
What  judges  look  for  Technical  execution  Does  it  work?  How  hard  was  it  to  build,  and  how  well  is  it  built?  Creativity  Is  the  idea  original,  or  a  fresh  take  on  a  known  problem?  Impact  Would  people  actually  use  this?  Does  it  solve  a  real  problem?  Demo  and  presentation  Can  you  clearly  explain  the  problem,  your  solution  and  how  it  works?
